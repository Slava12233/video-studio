# open/

One note per open item (`YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`), made from `memory/templates/open.md`: what waits, who acts (`owner: slava` for a click, a key, a go or a ruling; `owner: harness` for work the next session owes), the cost of waiting, links. `status: open` until it is done; then `status: done` with the date in the body (the note stays as the record; `/consolidate` archives it after 90 days). HOT and HOME list open items by owner. A go gate is not an open item: it is asked in chat when the estimate is ready.
